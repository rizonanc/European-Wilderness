# European Wilderness 2.12.50 for Minecraft 1.21.11

- European Wilderness branding across the world preset, settings and Explorer.
- Existing worlds keep their IDs. Settings and menu preferences migrate on first use.
- Fixes for snowy summits, Road Weaver tree placement and invalid precipitation predictions.
- Settings follow the game language. Experimental options remain available, and Reset section covers the entire section.

## Requirements and installation

Windows x64, Java 21, a DirectML-capable GPU, Fabric Loader 0.18.1 or newer and Fabric API. Put the JAR in `mods/` and select **European Wilderness** as the world type. Mod Menu is optional.

## Models

On first launch the mod downloads and verifies about 2.3 GB of [terrain models from Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/xandergos/terrain-diffusion-30m-onnx).
